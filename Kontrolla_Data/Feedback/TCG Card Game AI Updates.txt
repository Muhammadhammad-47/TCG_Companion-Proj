What are Kontrol/Control Cards?
Kontrol cards are Character Cards that allow you control or command any opponent’s actions. If successful in your Kontrol attack you may take any Action Card of your opponent or ask your opponent to attack another opponent.


How do I Kontrol/Control an opponent?
To control an opponent, you must first own a Kontrol Character Card of your character. When it is your turn. Combine your Attack Action Card with your character’s Kontrol Card. Whoever rolls the higher number dice will determine whether the attack is successful or not. This is best out of three rolls.


Can an opponent roll their own die to determine if a Kontrol attack is successful?
Yes, if the opponent has a Fate Card that allows you to roll a die to determine if the attack is successful or not. Every character’s mind strength is different from each other. Example, if a character has a 3 mind strength. He or she must roll a 4+ to defeat the mind control. If a character has 1. He or she must roll a 5+ to defeat the mind control.

Some Kontrol Cards come with an extra card that has a QR code. Is that card playable in this game?
Yes, this card is either a magnetic system that is attached with the Kontrol Card or it is in a hidden pouch. If your Kontrol attack is successful, the extra card allows the attacker to receive +20HP and -20HP on the opponent.


Why does Poochi have the same weaknesses as human characters such as Chynaman and Zabina “Bee” Solé?
Poochi carries two souls. When another magical force enters her body, the human soul and the monkey spirit fight for control. Because two magical energies exist inside her body, outside magic can disrupt her balance. Her magical body was once immune to poison, but the human soul changed her biology. She inherited the weaknesses of the soul she carries.
