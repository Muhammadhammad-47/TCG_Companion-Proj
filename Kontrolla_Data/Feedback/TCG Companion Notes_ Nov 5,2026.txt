Similar to Poison, here are the mechanics of Fire Attacks on Characters with Fire Weakness:


Character with Fire Weakness:
Initial damage: -10 HP from the Fire X1 or -20 HP from the Fire X2
Additional weakness penalty: -10 HP (one time)
Ongoing damage: -10 HP per turn on Fire X1 and -20 HP per turn on Fire X2
Continues until the player rolls a double on their next turn after the first initial attack.
