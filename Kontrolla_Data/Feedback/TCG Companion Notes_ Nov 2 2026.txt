How to use the RETREAT Card:


For Attacker:
When the attacker finishes his/her roll and is not confident that they can win the battle. The player may click on the button that says RETREAT (This option only appears if they have this card in their current deck) and that battle will automatically be over.


For Defender:
If the roll of the attacker is high (Ex: 10, 11, or 12) and the defender is not confident that he/she will win it, the player can opt out by clicking on the Retreat Card button (Similar to Defence Cards, if available) before they roll the dice and the battle will be over. 


***NOTE: If the defender uses the retreat card instead of battling, the player will lose one turn***


This is only valid if the Attacker or Defender has a Retreat Card.
