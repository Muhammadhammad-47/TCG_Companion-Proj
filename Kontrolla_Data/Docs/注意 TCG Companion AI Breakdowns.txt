1. GENERAL


What is the story of this game?
The universe we live in is imperial. A grand evil brewing about destroying worlds by sucking the life force and energy of all our worlds. Now your world is in danger of dying because not enough stability crystals to maintain its balance. It is your duty to save your world by obtaining three stability crystals by any means necessary. Whether you form alliances with other players or you must do devious treachery in order to obtain your crystals.


Where is the story and characters based on?
The story is based off the Anime Series: Attention 注意! that is now streaming on Youtube.


How do you decide which player starts first?
All players will roll two dice. Whoever rolls the highest number will start the game. If players roll the same number, they will continue to roll until one scores a higher number.


How do you begin the game?
* All players will roll two dice to determine who will start first. The player with the highest roll will be the first player and decide whether the rotation of players will be clockwise or counterclockwise
* The first player may be the card shuffler or nominate another player to shuffle the Action Card deck and pass out 10 cards to each player.
* Players will also choose one character to be for that round and have ten Character cards of the same character in their hands.
* Players will neither nor show anyone their Action Cards and Character Cards
* Each player will also begin with one Stability Crystal Card, which they will place in front of them. The goal is to defeat a player and collect their stability Crystal Card. The player who obtains three Stability Crystal Cards wins or ops out of the game and other players continue to battle until they win their three. If there are only two players. Two Stability Crystal Cards will be given to each player.
* The first player will claim the character he or she wishes to be then decide the order of player decisions will be clockwise or counter clockwise. 
* No player can be the same character, so once a character is chosen the other players must choose a different character to be.
* Each player will receive 5 energy tokens to start with and receive one extra energy token, only if they mention it. If it is not requested and the player's turn ends, they are no longer allowed to ask or receive an energy token until their next turn.
* The first  player will pick an Action Card and place it face up in the center of the players. He/She will then choose a character card to go along with the action they choose to make. In order for an action card to be valid, they must repent a character at the same time. Remember once the action is completed, the player must discard the Action Card and Character Card (Only if this applies)
* Once the Action or Character has been played and discarded. The player must grab another Action card from the top of the Action Card deck.. Players must also replace another Character Card if used. Players may choose which Character Card they would like to replace the discarded one.
* Players will always have 10 Action Cards and 10 Character Cards when playing. If the player uses two Action Cards in their turn, they must pick up two more Action Cards at the end of their turn.
* Each player will receive 5 energy tokens. Black = 1 energy token, Red = 2 energy token, Purple = 3 energy token. All players may receive 1 energy token every turn. However, no one will remind the player to get their energy token. Once their turn has passed, they can no longer get it.
* When a player is using an Attack Card, combined with a Character Card. The player must use an energy token.
* Regular attacks require 1 energy token. Super attacks require 2 energy tokens Kontrol requires 3 energy tokens and Blitz requires 5 energy tokens.
* Energy tokens may be traded to another player, if they are soul alliance or it is used as a trade for the Stability Crystal won from an Alliance battle.


How many Actions Cards do you pass out and have in hand at all times?
All players will have 10 Action Cards at all times. If a player uses an Action Card and discards it, he or she must grab another Action Card from the top of the Action Card deck.


Do I show players my Action Cards?
No, players do not reveal their Action Cards to others unless a Chance, Character, or Action Cards commands them to. The backs of the Action Cards will always face other players/opponents.


How many Character Cards do you pass out and have in hand at all times?
Players will always have 10 Character Cards in hand during the game. When one is discarded, the player will replenish it with their deck.


Can I choose which Character Cards I want to use?
At the beginning of the game, the player may choose which ten Character cards they wish to use, but afterwards must shuffle their Character deck and place them face down. When they pick another Character Card from the deck it must be the top one. Players can not shuffle the deck once the game has started.


How many Character Cards can you have in your Character Card deck?
Players may have as many Character Cards in their deck.


Can more than one player be the same character?
No, when a character is chosen by a player, that character is no longer available, until the game restarts.

What is Retreat?
Retreat is to run away from battle.


Do I need a Retreat Action Card to do this move?
Yes.


How does Retreat work?
The Retreat Action Card may be used during an attack or a while defending. 


When Attacking:
If you don’t like your attack roll, you may choose to Retreat before damage is resolved. The attack is then canceled, and you forfeit the battle, and your turn/attack ends. The Retreat card must be presented immediately after the Attacker rolls. Once the defender begins to roll their dice, this move is no longer valid.


When Defending:
After an opponent declares an attack but before damage is calculated, you may Retreat instead of resolving the battle. Your character escapes and takes 0 damage. The Retreat Card must be played before the defender rolls their dice.


Every Character has a different Retreat Speed, which is located on single attack Character Cards. They are not present on Wild Cards, Kontrol, and No Saigo Blitz.

What happens if a player does not roll the necessary number to Retreat?
If the player is unsuccessful with the Retreat, the player must continue on the battle whether you are attacking or defending.

3. ENERGY TOKEN:


Energy Tokens (ET): Black = 1 ET/ Red = 2 ET/ Gold = 3ET 


What are Energy Tokens?
Energy Tokens represent the power needed to perform an attack. Players spend Energy Tokens to activate Attack Cards and special offensive abilities.


What color is 1 ET?
Black


What color is 2 ET?
Red


What color is 3 ET?
Gold


How many energy tokens do each player start out in the game?
Each player receives five energy tokens at the beginning of each game.


Does a player receive an extra energy token after the game begins?
Yes, players do receive one energy token after each turn. However, players must request to be given one energy token.


Can I claim my energy token after my turn is over?
No, players can not receive your energy token after their turn is over. The player must wait for their next turn to receive another energy token.


Do I need Energy Tokens if I am defending?
No, Energy Tokens are only needed when attacking. Energy Tokens are also not needed for defense, healing, retreating, or other actions unless a card specially allows it.

Players are required to use one Energy Token to activate this attack.

How many Energy Tokens do I need to perform a Blitz Attack?

How to attack?
One vs. One Attack. 
Whomever rolls the highest number on the dice wins. Both Attacker and Defender will use two dice to roll.


One vs. All Attack:
Whomever rolls the highest number on the dice will win. Attackers will roll against all other players. Defenders who are on the roll will dodge the blast/attack. Defenders who lose will take damage.


How is damage dealt in One vs. All Attack?
All defenders that lost on the roll will divide the amount of 200 AP equally. For example, 5 defenders lose. Each one will be dealt -40 HP. 200 AP / 5 losing defender = -40 HP to each losing defender.. 2 defenders lose. Each one will be dealt -100 HP. 200 AP/ 2 losing defender = -100 HP  each losing defender

When a Zombie is successful in its attack, it will inflict Poison to their opponent, which results in the defeated opponent to receive one Poison Card.

If Zombie HP is depleted to 0, then…
Zombies will revive at 10 HP (5 Poison Cards)
Zombies will revive at 20 HP (6+ Poison Cards)
Zombies will revive at 40 HP (8+ Poison Cards)


Zombies can infect other players just by successfully attacking another opponent. If the Zombie is successful in its attack, the defendant will receive one Poison card and follow the rules of the Poison card. Zombies can not be killed by regular and BLITZ Character Card attacks and even if a Character Card destroys them, they will come back to life. Zombies receive +10 HP each round regardless if they take damage or not. Kontrol Card will not work on Zombies since they have no mind.


Once another player takes off one Poison card from the total of five Poison Cards, the infected player is no longer a zombie. They will discard the Zombie Mode Card to the current dealer and go back to the HP they had before they turned into a Zombie. All Poison Cards at this time are still valid and will continue to take effect for as long as they have it.


Fire attacks, Fire Cards, Lightning Cards, or Lightning Attacks will remove a Poison card when the infected player is in Zombie Mode, but will not remove a Poison Card in regular mode. The Fire and Lighting attack will still deal damage.
Zombie Mode
When a player has 5 Poison Cards, they immediately enter Zombie Mode.
The dealer gives that player a Zombie Mode Card, which must be placed face-up in front of them so all players know they are infected.
While in Zombie Mode, the following rules apply:
Character Restrictions
* The player cannot use any Character Cards or Character Card abilities.
* All attacks must come from the Zombie Mode Card.
* Character-specific skills, passive effects, transformations, and ultimate attacks are disabled while in Zombie Mode.
Winning Restrictions
* A player in Zombie Mode cannot claim or win a Stability Crystal Card.
* If a Zombie would earn a Stability Crystal Card, they cannot take it until they are cured and return to Regular Mode.

Zombie Stats
Starting HP: 40 HP
* Zombie HP may still increase through Vitality Cards, Heal Cards, or other effects that restore HP.
* At the beginning of each of the Zombie player's turns, they automatically recover +10 HP, even if they took damage during the previous round.
Zombie Attack - Venom Strike
The Zombie's primary attack is Venom Strike.
If Venom Strike successfully defeats or damages an opponent according to your game's attack rules:
* The defending player immediately receives 1 Poison Card.
* That Poison Card follows all normal Poison Card rules.
A player with Poison Cards continues to lose 10 HP or 20 HP each round, depending on the Poison Card's effect, until they use an Antidote Card.

Zombies spread the virus through combat.

* The defending player gains 1 Poison Card.

8. TERMINOLOGY


General:
注意 : Attention
TCG: Trading Card Game
Stacking: Placing the same card on top of each other or using the same card at the same time during the match.
HP (Health Points): The amount of life or vitality a player or character has. HP is reduced when damage is taken from attacks or effects. When a player’s HP reaches 0, they are defeated unless they have or is given a Revive card.
AP (Attack Power): The amount of damage an attack deals to an opponent when the attack is successful.
DP (Defense Power): A value that represents a player’s or character’s defensive strength, helping reduce or withstand incoming attacks.
RP (Retreat Points): Represents a character’s ability to escape combat or reposition. The higher a character’s RP is the lower number a player has to roll. Example, if a character’s RP is 5, the player will only need to roll a 1. Only dice is used to determine whether the player can retreat or must stay and fight. If a character has a RP of 2, the player must roll 4+ on one dice. Players must have a Retreat Card in order to retreat. 
ET (Energy Token): A resource used to activate attacks and special abilities. Energy Tokens are spent when required by a card’s effect or The spiritual force that fuels a warrior’s techniques. Spend Energy Tokens to unleash powerful attacks and devastating abilities.
Weakness: Attacks matching its weakness gain a bonus effect, such as extra damage, status conditions, or special penalties.


Card Types
Action: These cards determine the moves or action such as to Attack, Defend, Heal, or any other moves.
Character: These cards determine the type of attack a player can do. Each character has different powers and abilities. Some cards are more common than others.
Chance: These cards test your fate. Players may receive good cards or bad cards depending on their luck.


Turn Actions
Activate: To enable the ability to… (Defend, Cast a spell, etc)


LEVEL UP:
Level UP: Players may not use their level 2 cards until they raise their health point to 150. Once a player has leveled up to 2+ He or she may use their level 1 attacks. Their HP will not go down unless they are attacked


Ability:
Ability: A unique effect granted by a card that can influence gameplay.
Passive Ability: An ability that remains active without requiring activation unless otherwise stated.
Active Ability: An ability that must be activated by meeting its requirements or paying its cost.
Status Effect: A temporary condition, such as Poison, Burn, or Stun, that alters a player's or character's abilities.
Cooldown: The number of turns a player must wait before using the same ability or card again.
Turn: A player's opportunity to perform actions according to the game's turn sequence.
Round: A complete cycle in which every player has taken one turn.
Card Effect: The special rules or abilities printed on a card.
Successful Attack: An attack that meets all requirements to hit and applies its effects.
Miss: An attack that fails to hit its target and does not deal damage unless stated otherwise.
Critical Hit: An attack that deals additional damage or gains an enhanced effect when specific conditions are met.
Combo: A sequence of attacks or abilities that work together to create a stronger effect.
Counter: A defensive response that interrupts, redirects, or retaliates against an opponent's action.
Redirect: Changes the target of an attack or ability to another legal target.
Heal: Restores lost HP. A player's HP cannot exceed its maximum value unless a card effect states otherwise.
Victory: A player has won 3 Stability Crystals and has saved their world from destruction.
Status Effect: A temporary or ongoing condition placed on a character that causes an additional effect beyond normal HP damage.

What are Poison Cards?
Poison Cards are Action Cards that deal damage to an opponent each turn until they are cured.


Do I have to roll dice to activate a Poison Card?
No, Poison Cards do not require dice rolls.

Players may have an unlimited amount of Poison Cards during each game, but is limited to the amount of Poison Cards in the Action Card deck.

Yes, a player may use a Poison Card on themselves to transform themselves into a Zombie.
