***Slight Changes in the Mechanics of the beginning of the game. Because some characters are
weaker than others. I have designed this new mechanic to help balance things out.***
The system is called: RESERVE ENERGY RECOVERY
How does it work?
Characters with lower starting HP receive a reserve HP supply that they can activate whenever
they choose.
Every activation costs 2 Energy Tokens, regardless of how much reserve HP they choose to
recover.
For Characters with higher HP their Reserve Energy Recovery is dormant, until they receive a
special rare Character Card which unlocks this ability.

Reserve Energy Unlock Card
Expansion Pack mechanic
Purpose: Unlocks a character's hidden Reserve Energy Recovery ability.
Proposed mechanics
1. Only eligible characters can use the card.
2. Playing the card unlocks their Reserve Energy.
3. Each recovery activation costs 2 Energy Tokens.
4. Players choose how much reserve HP to recover.
5. Recovery cannot exceed the character's current maximum HP.
6. Reserve Energy cannot be transferred to another character. You can introduce stronger characters with:
● Higher starting HP.
● Larger Reserve Energy capacity.
● Unique recovery abilities.
● Special Vitality Cards.
● New character-specific moves.
This also gives you opportunities to introduce rare or legendary Reserve Energy cards.

How I would structure it
Each character has different versions or evolutions of their Character Card.
Level 1 Character Card
Common
● Starting HP
● Basic Defense
● Level 1 moves
● No Reserve Energy Recovery
Level 2 Character Card
Rare ● Higher starting HP
● Level 2 moves
● Character-specific moves
● Potential Reserve Energy Recovery unlock
Advanced Character Card
Legendary
● Higher-level abilities
● Expanded Reserve Energy capacity
● Special recovery abilities
Example:
Imagine a player has Shroomy at Level 1 with 65 HP.
They purchase an expansion booster pack and discover a rare Shroomy Character Card
containing:
Reserve Energy Recovery: Unlocked
● Reserve capacity: 35 HP at Level 1.
● Each activation costs 2 Energy Tokens.
● The player chooses how much HP to recover.
● Maximum HP remains subject to the character's current level.
The rare card becomes something players actively hunt for.
Character Add-On Cards
Expansion pack feature
The original Character Card remains unchanged. Players must discover a rare Add-On Card in
an expansion booster pack to unlock that character's Reserve Energy Recovery ability.
Example: Shroomy
Original Character Card
● Starting HP: 65
● Reserve HP: 35
● Reserve recovery: Locked
Rare Add-On Card
● Unlocks Reserve Energy Recovery. ● Each activation costs 2 Energy Tokens.
● The player chooses the recovery amount.
● Reserve capacity: 35 HP at Level 1.
Combined Character Abilities
Original character + Rare Add-On = Reserve Energy Recovery unlocked.
Why this is a smart business decision
1. You don't need to reprint existing Character Cards.
2. Expansion packs introduce new collectible values.
3. Players have incentives to purchase additional booster packs.
4. You can introduce different Add-On abilities in future expansions.
5. Your original game remains compatible with future releases.
One rule I'd establish: The Add-On Card should remain attached to the character or placed
beside it while active. If that character is defeated, the Add-On returns to the player's collection
rather than being destroyed.
